Levy allowance now

Approving is not a payment. It sets how much Levy may collect for your subscriptions over time, and you can lower it whenever you like.

How the allowance works
  • A ceiling, not a charge. The approval lets the Levy contract take USDC from your wallet up to the amount you set. Nothing moves until a charge is due.
  • One allowance for all your Levy subscriptions. Every charge uses up part of it, so checkout adds this plan's cycles to what you have already approved instead of replacing it.
  • Only the plan's price, once per cycle, to this merchant. The contract enforces this for every subscription. Nobody can raise the price or charge early, Levy included.
  • When it runs low, the portal warns you and offers a top-up. If it runs out, charges fail, and after repeated failures the subscription pauses.
  • To lower or remove it, set a new USDC approval for the Levy contract (shown above) from your wallet. To stop paying, cancel in the portal.
